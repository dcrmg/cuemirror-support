用法说明

首页

iPhone 与 iPad · iOS 17 或更高版本

提词器相机是一个对着镜头读词的录像应用。打开就是相机。台词只出现在屏幕上，不会出现在成片里。

怎么开始拍

  1. 点「稿子」，选一篇或自己写。空一行表示停一下。
  2. 把提词框拖到镜头旁边，字号用两指捏合调到看得清。
  3. 点红色拍摄键。录的时候，最右边变成暂停，画面和滚动一起停，再点一次一起继续。
  4. 允许相册后，拍完的视频只保存在系统相册，「成片」打开的就是这一份。不授权也可以拍，视频会留在成片里，一样可以查看。

底部五个按钮是稿子、设置、拍摄、切换摄像头、成片。宽屏幕上，拍摄键在右侧，稿子和设置在左下。

三种滚动

  • 匀速：按你设定的每分钟字数往下走。
  • 定时：在你设定的时间里读完整篇。
  • AI 跟读：说到哪，字跟到哪。你一停，字也停。需要允许语音识别。设备支持时在本机识别。

拍口播时，优先用 AI 跟读。不要死盯着字，瞄半句，再对着镜头说。

视线、倒计时和旋转

视线是眼睛该看的高度，正在读的那一行会加重。倒计时可以是 0、3、5 或 10 秒。横着拿手机时，用「旋转文字」把字转正。竖拍保存竖屏，横拍保存横屏。

前置用来拍自己，预览像照镜子，录进视频的画面仍然是正的。后置拍前面的人和景物。还没录像时，点一下画面会对焦，稿子也会开始滚或停住。正在录像时，点一下只对焦。人脸会优先对上焦，也优先照顾曝光。

麦克风和拍完确认

连上蓝牙麦克风后默认用它录音，在设置里关掉就用手机麦克风。打开「拍摄后确认」后，拍完可以重拍或留下。

试用和解锁

第一次打开后有 3 天免费试用。试用结束后，继续录制需要付费解锁。这是一次性购买，换设备可以用「恢复购买」。试用期内，设置里能看到试用到什么时候。

删除

允许了相册时，在「成片」里删除会同时删除系统相册里的那个视频。没允许相册时，只删除成片里的这一份。

权限

  • 相机：预览和录像。
  • 麦克风：录下声音。
  • 语音识别：仅 AI 跟读需要。
  • 相册：可选。允许后，视频只保存在系统相册，成片打开的就是这一份。不授权也可以拍摄。
  • 位置：可选。允许后，拍摄地点会记在视频里。不授权也能拍。

还是不行时

相机打不开时，到系统设置里打开相机和麦克风。AI 跟读没有反应时，打开语音识别。允许了相册却仍看不到系统相册里的视频时，把相册权限设为允许访问，而不是仅添加。

Teleprompter Camera is a recorder for reading to the lens. The camera is the home screen. The script stays on the display and is never burned into the video.

Record a take

  1. Open Scripts and pick one, or write your own. A blank line is a pause.
  2. Drag the script box next to the lens. Pinch with two fingers until the type is easy to read.
  3. Tap the red record button. While recording, the rightmost button becomes Pause. Video and scrolling stop together, and the next tap continues both.
  4. With Photos access, the finished video stays only in Photos, and Clips opens that copy. You can record without Photos access; the video then stays in Clips and can still be played.

On a phone, the five controls are Scripts, Settings, Record, Flip, and Clips. On a wide screen, Record moves to the right, and Scripts and Settings stay at the lower left.

Three ways to scroll

  • Steady moves at the characters-per-minute pace you set.
  • Timer finishes the script in the length of time you set.
  • AI Follow moves with your voice and stops when you stop. It needs speech recognition. Recognition stays on the device when the device supports it.

For a talking video, start with AI Follow. Don’t stare at the words. Glance at half a sentence, then say it to the lens.

Eyeline, countdown, and rotation

The eyeline is the height your eyes should use. The line you are reading is heavier. Countdown can be 0, 3, 5, or 10 seconds. When you hold the phone sideways, use Rotate to turn the words upright. Portrait filming is saved portrait. Landscape filming is saved landscape.

The front camera is for filming yourself. The preview is mirrored, and the recorded picture is not. The back camera films what is in front of you. Before recording, a tap focuses and also starts or pauses the scroll. During recording, a tap only focuses. A face is preferred for focus and exposure.

Microphone and review

A connected Bluetooth mic is used for audio. Turn that setting off to use the iPhone mic. With Review after recording on, you can retake or keep the clip.

Trial and unlock

The first open starts a 3-day free trial. After that, recording requires a one-time purchase. On another device, use Restore purchases. During the trial, Settings shows when it ends.

Delete

If Photos access is on, deleting a clip also deletes that video in Photos. If it is off, only the copy in Clips is deleted.

Permissions

  • Camera: preview and record.
  • Microphone: record audio.
  • Speech recognition: required only for AI Follow.
  • Photos: optional. If allowed, videos stay only in Photos and Clips opens that copy. Recording works without it.
  • Location: optional. If allowed, the filming place is stored in the video. Recording works without it.

If something fails

If the camera does not open, allow Camera and Microphone in Settings. If AI Follow does nothing, allow Speech Recognition. If Photos access is on but Clips cannot open the album video, set Photos to full access rather than add-only.
