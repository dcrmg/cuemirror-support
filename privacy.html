本政策说明 iOS 应用「提词器相机」（英文名 Teleprompter Camera）如何处理信息。开发者没有自己的服务器，不会接收你的视频、录音或人脸数据。

生效日期：2026年10月22日。

我们不收集的内容

  • 不要求注册账号，不读取通讯录、日历或广告标识。
  • 不接入分析、广告或追踪工具，不把数据出售给任何人。
  • 不把视频、声音或人脸数据上传到开发者的服务器。开发者没有这类服务器。

相机与麦克风

相机用来预览和录像。麦克风用来给视频录音。台词只显示在屏幕上，不会烧进成片。录像在你的设备上完成。你可以拒绝这两项权限，但那样就无法拍摄。

人脸数据

拍摄时，系统相机会做人脸检测，用来让人脸优先对焦、优先曝光，包括逆光时尽量把脸拍清楚。这是 iOS 相机框架在本机完成的处理，不是人脸识别，也不生成面纹或人脸模板。

具体而言：

  • 人脸检测只用于当前画面的对焦和曝光，不用来判断你是谁。
  • 开发者不采集、不保存、不上传人脸几何、人脸特征或单独的人脸图片。
  • 检测结果不单独留存。画面处理过后，除了你选择保存的视频本身，没有另一份人脸数据。
  • 你的脸如果出现在画面里，只是视频内容的一部分。视频保存在你的系统相册。你在相册或本应用的「成片」里删除后，该画面一并删除。
  • 人脸数据不用于广告、追踪，也不与第三方共享。

本应用不使用 TrueDepth 面容网格，也不建立可供识别身份的人脸数据库。

语音识别（AI 跟读）

只有在你使用 AI 跟读时，应用才会把正在说的话交给语音识别，用来让字幕跟着你的声音滚动。设备支持本地识别时，识别在这台 iPhone 或 iPad 上完成。设备不支持本地识别时，系统会把该段声音发给 Apple 的语音识别服务，只为完成这一次识别。开发者收不到这段声音，也不保存识别文本。

你可以拒绝语音识别。拒绝后不能使用 AI 跟读，匀速和定时仍然可以拍摄。

相册

相册权限可以选择不给，不给也可以拍摄。允许之后，视频只保存在系统相册，「成片」打开、播放和删除的就是这一份，应用不再另存。你在「成片」里删除时，相册中的对应文件也会删除。

没有允许相册时，视频保存在「成片」里，可以显示和查看，不会上传。之后如果补开了相册权限，这些已拍的视频会移入系统相册，应用里不再留一份。

位置

若你允许位置权限，拍摄地点会写进这条视频，系统相册也可以显示该位置。位置不会发给开发者。不允许位置也可以正常拍摄，只是视频里没有地点。

蓝牙麦克风

连接蓝牙麦克风时，系统会改用该麦克风录音。应用不扫描周围设备清单，不读取蓝牙联系人，也不把设备标识上传。

购买

新安装后有 3 天免费试用。试用结束后，继续录制需要一次性内购解锁。付款由 Apple 处理。开发者看不到你的信用卡号、账单地址或 Apple ID 密码，只能从 Apple 得知这项解锁是否已购买。

保存在本机的内容

稿子、拍摄设置和试用起始时间保存在这台设备上，用于让应用能继续工作。这些内容不会上传。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会明知地收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如果处理方式有实质变化，会更新本页顶部的日期。继续使用应用即表示你了解更新后的政策。

This policy explains how the iOS app Teleprompter Camera (提词器相机) handles information. The developer does not run a server that receives your videos, recordings, or face data.

Effective date: October 22, 2026.

What we do not collect

  • No account, and no access to your contacts, calendar, or advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not sell data.
  • Videos, audio, and face data are not uploaded to a developer server. There is no such server.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used to preview and record. The microphone records the soundtrack. The script stays on screen and is not burned into the video. Recording happens on your device. You can deny either permission, and then recording will not be available.

Face data

While you film, the system camera detects a face so it can focus on that face and expose for it, including in backlight. This is on-device processing by the iOS camera framework. It is not facial recognition, and it does not create a faceprint or a face template.

In particular:

  • Face detection is used only to focus and expose the current frame. It is not used to identify who you are.
  • The developer does not collect, store, or upload face geometry, facial features, or a separate face image.
  • Detection results are not kept on their own. After a frame is processed, the only image that remains is the video you chose to save.
  • If your face appears, it is part of that video. The video is stored in your Photos library. Deleting it in Photos, or in Clips inside the app, deletes that picture as well.
  • Face data is not used for advertising or tracking, and it is not shared with third parties.

The app does not use the TrueDepth face mesh, and it does not build a face database that could identify a person.

Speech recognition (AI Follow)

Speech recognition runs only while you use AI Follow, so the script can move with your voice. When the device supports on-device recognition, it stays on that iPhone or iPad. When it does not, the system sends that audio to Apple’s speech service for that recognition only. The developer does not receive the audio and does not store the transcript.

You can deny speech recognition. AI Follow will be unavailable. Steady and Timer recording still work.

Photos

Photos access is optional, and recording works without it. If you allow it, videos stay only in Photos. Clips opens, plays, and deletes that same copy, and the app does not keep a second file. Deleting a clip also deletes the Photos asset.

Without Photos access, the video stays in Clips, where it can be viewed, and it is not uploaded. If you allow Photos later, those existing videos move into Photos and the app copy is removed.

Location

If you allow location, the filming location is written into that video, and Photos can show it. The location is not sent to the developer. You can record without location. The video then has no place attached.

Bluetooth microphones

When a Bluetooth microphone is connected, the system uses it for the recording. The app does not scan a list of nearby devices, read Bluetooth contacts, or upload a device identifier.

Purchases

A new install includes a 3-day free trial. After that, recording requires a one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment. The developer never sees your card number, billing address, or Apple ID password, and only learns from Apple whether the unlock has been purchased.

What stays on the device

Scripts, camera settings, and the trial start time stay on the device so the app can keep working. They are not uploaded.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the way information is handled changes in a material way, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continuing to use the app means you have seen the updated policy.
